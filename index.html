Pond Rescue

Two fish ponds, one aerator motor and four days of sewage spills. Write Python to keep both ponds clean without setting the motor on fire.

ReadyPress Run to try the starter code.

Your functions

        

          
pollution(pond)
Pollution in pond 1 or 2. Below 3 is safe. Above 8 the fish die.

          
motor_temp()
Motor temperature in °C. At 100°C it catches fire.

          
motor_on()
Starts the motor. Air goes to one pond and cleans it. Heats up about 15°C an hour.

          
motor_off()
Stops the motor. Cools down about 8°C an hour.

          
switch_to(pond)
Moves the air hose to pond 1 or 2. Takes 3 hours. The motor must be off first!

          
wait()
Lets 3 hours pass. The sensors only update every 3 hours, so to wait longer use wait(6), wait(9)…

        

        

          
The hose starts in pond 1. The motor starts off at 20°C.

          
Sewage builds up slowly. Toxic spills arrive suddenly and keep leaking for hours.

          
The run lasts 4 days. On screen, 1 second is 1 hour.
